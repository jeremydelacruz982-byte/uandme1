      Step 5 of 6

      Before you send it

      Here's everything as they'll see it.

      This is just between the two of you — no one else can see it, and it disappears after 90 days.

      What that means, in detail This link and any replies to it live in temporary storage rather than a permanent account or database, and are set to expire automatically. Anyone who has the link can open it, so only share it with the person it's for. Don't use this for anything you wouldn't want the recipient to screenshot and keep — once it's sent, you can't unsend it.

      In this preview build, replies are stored in this browser only, so the private link only shows replies submitted from the same device. A live deployment replaces that local step with the ephemeral, auto-expiring store described in the architecture — same flow, real cross-device delivery.
